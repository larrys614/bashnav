                Export is the backup
                
      
                The log lives in this browser's storage on this device. That is
        reliable, but it is per-device, invisible, and you can erase it
        by clearing website data. A boat's record cannot live only there.
                
      
                
      
                
      
                
        
        
      
                
      
                
    
                

    
                
      
                Import never replaces. It merges.
                
      
                Replacing a log with an imported one is the most destructive thing this
        app could do, and it is one mis-tap from losing a passage. So there is no
        replace. Nothing is written until you have seen the preview.
                
      
      
                
    
                
  
                

  
  
                
    
                
      
                What this is
                
      
                The boat's records: the deck log, weather, engine and provisions, in one
        tool, prompting every three hours. It shares one log with the POSIX
        awk build — the same file, byte for byte,
        not two formats that look alike.
                
      
                Three-hourly on the clock, because that gives the real three-hour
        pressure characteristic and includes all four synoptic hours
        (0000/0600/1200/1800 UTC) that forecast verification needs. The
        observation cadence is the app's, not the watch's.
                
    
                

    
                
      
                A log is a record, not a notes file
                
      
                Append only. Nothing is ever edited in place or deleted
        — not by convention, but because the storage itself only appends.
                
      
                Corrections are new records. The maritime convention is
        that you do not erase a log: you line through, initial, and write the
        correction. The electronic equivalent keeps both visible for ever.
                
      
                UTC always. Display in whatever you like; the record is
        UTC.
                
    
                

    
                
      
                Four states, not two
                
      
                  
        
                • 1013.2observed, and here it is
                  • 
        
                • 0observed, and the answer was none. Zero is a reading.
                  • 
        
                • –asked, and not taken
                  • 
        
                • /asked, and not observable — dark, fog, obscured
                  • 
        
                • absentnever asked: the section was declined, or this version had no such field
                  • 
      
                
      
                A form that punishes blanks gets invented numbers. Somebody
        who did not read the barometer writes something plausible rather than
        leave a gap that looks like negligence. Make “I did not observe
        this” a first-class, recorded, unremarkable answer and people use it
        honestly.
                
    
                

    
                
      
                True wind, never apparent
                
      
                On a sailing boat apparent wind changes every time you alter course. Log
        apparent and the backing-and-veering analysis reports a dramatic veer on
        every tack and a backing breeze on every gybe — and that is the most
        useful rule there is. It would have been noise, and plausible-looking
        noise. So the log stores true wind, with course and speed
        at the same instant so the correction can be checked.
                
    
                

    
                
      
                Carried forward, and not
                
      
                State persists; a measurement does not. At 1800 the
        course really is what it was at 1500 unless somebody changed it, so it is
        shown as a default. The pressure at 1800 is not the 1500 reading
        unless changed — it is a new reading or it is nothing, so no default
        is offered at all. Had carry-forward applied to everything, the log would
        have grown a column of barometer readings nobody took: worse than a gap,
        because it looks like data.
                
    
                

    
                
      
                Who may write to it
                
      
                The log records what happened on this boat, and nothing else may write to
        it. A real sight and a real fix, an observed tide height, a contact
        plotted for a real CPA, anything entered at the nav desk — yes. Every
        drill, quiz, scenario and sandbox sight — never. A log containing
        invented vessels from a training scenario is not untidy, it is dangerous.

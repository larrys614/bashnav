=== reflights ===

  LIGHTS -- the whole set
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  power50        Power-driven vessel under 50 m, under way
                 Rule 23  One masthead light, sidelights, sternlight.
  power50p       Power-driven vessel of 50 m or more, under way
                 Rule 23  Two masthead lights, the after one higher.
  sail           Sailing vessel under way
                 Rule 25  Sidelights and a sternlight. No masthead light - that is the whole point.
  sailrg         Sailing vessel, optional all-round pair
                 Rule 25  Red over green at the masthead, with normal sidelights and sternlight.
  anchor         Vessel at anchor, under 50 m
                 Rule 30  A single all-round white light where it can best be seen.
  anchor50       Vessel at anchor, 50 m or more
                 Rule 30  Two all-round whites, forward one higher, and the decks lit.
  aground        Vessel aground
                 Rule 30  Anchor light or lights, plus two all-round reds in a vertical line.
  nuc            Not under command, making no way
                 Rule 27  Two all-round reds. Nothing else if she is not moving through the water.
  nucway         Not under command, making way
                 Rule 27  Two all-round reds, and because she is making way, sidelights and sternlight too.
  ram            Restricted in her ability to manoeuvre
                 Rule 27  Red, white, red in a vertical line. Making way, so the ordinary lights as well.
  draught        Constrained by her draught
                 Rule 28  Three all-round reds in a vertical line, over the ordinary power-driven lights.
  trawl          Vessel trawling
                 Rule 26  Green over white. Green for a trawl dragged along the bottom.
  fishing        Fishing, other than trawling
                 Rule 26  Red over white. The old rhyme: red over white, fishing at night.
  pilot          Pilot vessel on duty
                 Rule 29  White over red: pilot ahead. Sidelights and sternlight when under way.
  tow200         Towing, tow under 200 m
                 Rule 24  Two masthead lights in a vertical line, and a yellow towing light above the sternlight.
  tow200p        Towing, tow over 200 m
                 Rule 24  Three masthead lights in a vertical line - the tow is longer than 200 m.
  towed          Vessel being towed
                 Rule 24  Sidelights and a sternlight, and nothing else. She looks like a sailing vessel until you see what is ahead of her.
  mineclear      Mine clearance vessel
                 Rule 27  Three all-round greens: one at the foremast head and one at each end of the fore yard. Keep 1000 m clear.
  hover          Air-cushion vessel in non-displacement mode
                 Rule 23  The ordinary power-driven lights plus an all-round flashing yellow.
  sail7          Sailing vessel under 7 m, or a vessel under oars
                 Rule 25  If she carries nothing else she must show a torch or lantern in time to prevent collision.
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Show any one from any angle:  colregs light <key> <bearing>

=== cref ===

  CONTACTS -- the card
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Away from your bow  ->  she passes ASTERN. Always. No exceptions.
  Toward your bow     ->  she CROSSES AHEAD. The miss is in front
                          of you, and it shrinks as the drift slows.
  No drift at all     ->  collision. Rule 7(d)(i). Deemed, not judged.

  On the left, drawing left ... she goes astern.
  On the left, drawing right ... she crosses your bow.
  On the right, drawing right ... astern.  Drawing left ... ahead.

  Risk anyway, even with good drift -- Rule 7(d)(ii):
    a very large vessel   you plotted her bridge, her bow is nearer
    a tow                 the tug clears, the barge astern does not
    close range           drift goes as range squared

  Report:  Master 2, bearing 311, Red 20, drawing left,
           range 14,200, CPA 4,100 yards at 18 minutes.
           Bearing before drift. Say "steady" out loud.
           True for the plot, relative for the eye.

  Relative:  Red = port, Green = starboard, 0 to 180 each side.
    000 right ahead   ~35 fine on the bow   045 broad on the bow
    090 on the beam   135 broad on the quarter   180 right astern
  Relative bearings move when YOU turn. Drift is measured in
  TRUE bearings, or on a steady course. Never across a turn.

  3 min : yards = knots x 100      6 min : miles = knots / 10
  time to CPA = range / closing rate per minute
  CPA  =  miles  x  degrees left to drift  x  35
  Ekelund kyd = 1.91 x change in speed across / change in rate
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

=== scenlights 1 ===

  THE LIGHTS
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  She bears 015 T, 003 relative -- right ahead, 8.7 nm.
  You see   red port light, under red over white -- engaged in fishing
  She sees  green starboard light
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  The all-round lights outrank the colour. Red over white is the signal
  that governs: you keep out of the way of a vessel engaged in fishing
  (Rule 18), even when she is showing you green.

  What the colour is worth: it decides a power-driven crossing and
  nothing else. An all-round light above a sidelight outranks it, a
  sailing vessel's right of way is decided by tack rather than by
  aspect, and head-on or overtaking are settled before you look at the
  colour at all.

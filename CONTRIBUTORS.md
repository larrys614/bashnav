# Contributors

People who have checked the claims in these programs against the rules,
the almanac or a tide table, and told us what was wrong.

Credit here is **opt-in and by name only**. No email address is collected
by the tools, stored in this repository, or published here. `colregs
review` asks whether you want to be credited and what name to use; say no,
or say nothing, and nothing is recorded. Ask at any time and your name
comes out.

If a correction of yours lands, you belong on this list — a name in the
issue is not the same as consent to publish it, so we ask.

## How to get here

Run `colregs review`, work through the claims, and send the report. It
builds a link that opens a GitHub issue with your findings already in it.
The tools never touch the network: you open the link, and GitHub signs the
issue with your own account, so nothing is anonymous and there is no
address for anyone to collect.

See also `tests/REVIEW.md` for what is worth checking and in what order.
